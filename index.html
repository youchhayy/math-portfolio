Notation:

  • \(P\): Applied load at the end of the beam
  • \(w\): Distributed load of the beam, or beam weight
  • \(s\): Arc length from root \((s=0)\) to tip \((s=L)\)
  • \(u\): Dummy variable for distributed load of the beam
  • \(x(s), x(u)\): Horizontal coordinates
  • \(x_L\): Final horizontal tip coordinate, \(x_L=x(L)\)

At small angle of \(\theta\), we get,

\[ \frac{dx}{ds}=\cos\theta \quad \text{and} \quad \frac{dy}{ds}=\sin\theta \]

And the curvature of the beam is

\[ \kappa=\frac{d\theta}{ds} \]

Then the moment at arc length \(s\),

\[ M(s)=EI\frac{d\theta}{ds} \]

Differentiating with respect to \(s\):

\[ \frac{d^2\theta}{ds^2} = \frac{1}{EI}\frac{dM}{ds} \]

Moment that acts on its own weight from \(s\) to \(L\):

\[ M_{\text{weight}} = \int_s^L w[x(u)-x(s)]\,du \]

Moment at point load \(P\):

\[ M_{\text{tip}} = P[x_L-x(s)] \]

Total Moment Equation is

\[ M(s) = \int_s^L w[x(u)-x(s)]\,du + P[x_L-x(s)] \]

Differentiate \(M(s)\) with respect to \(s\) using the Leibniz integral rule:

\[ \frac{dM}{ds} = \frac{d}{ds} \left[ \int_s^L w[x(u)-x(s)]\,du \right] + \frac{d}{ds} \left[ P[x_L-x(s)] \right] \]
\[ = -w(L-s)\frac{dx}{ds} - P\frac{dx}{ds} \]
\[ = -w(L-s)\cos\theta - P\cos\theta \]
\[ = [-w(L-s)-P]\cos\theta \]

Substitute \(\frac{dM}{ds}\) back into \(\frac{d^2\theta}{ds^2}\), we get,

\[ \frac{d^2\theta}{ds^2} = \frac{1}{EI} [-w(L-s)-P]\cos\theta \]

The initial conditions are

\[ \theta(0)=0 \]
\[ \left(\frac{d\theta}{ds}\right)_{s=L}=0 \]
\[ (\delta_x)_{s=L}=L-x_L \]
\[ (\delta_y)_{s=L}=x_L \]
