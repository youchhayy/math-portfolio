Notation:

  • \(P\): Apply load at the end of the beam
  • \(w\): Distribution load of the beam, or beam weight
  • \(s\): Arc length from root \((s=0)\) to tip \((s=L)\)
  • \(u\): Dummy variable for distribution load of the beam
  • \(x(s), x(u)\): Horizontal coordinates
  • \(x_L\): Final horizontal tip coordinate, \(x_L=x(L)\)

At small angle of \(\theta\), we get,

\[ \frac{dx}{ds}=\cos\theta \quad \text{and} \quad \frac{dy}{ds}=\sin\theta \]

And the curvature of the beam is

\[ \kappa=\frac{d\theta}{ds} \]

Then the moment at arc length \(s\),

\[ M(s)=EI\frac{d\theta}{ds} \]

Differentiating with respect to \(s\):

\[ \frac{d^2\theta}{ds^2} = \frac{1}{EI}\frac{dM}{ds} \]

Moment that acts on its own weight from \(s\) to \(L\):

\[ M_{\text{weight}} = \int_s^L w[x(u)-x(s)]\,du \]

Moment at point load \(P\):

\[ M_{\text{tip}} = P[x_L-x(s)] \]

Total Moment Equation is

\[ M(s) = \int_s^L w[x(u)-x(s)]\,du + P[x_L-x(s)] \]

Differentiate \(M(s)\) with respect to \(s\) using the Leibniz integral rule:

\[ \frac{dM}{ds} = \frac{d}{ds} \left[ \int_s^L w[x(u)-x(s)]\,du \right] + \frac{d}{ds} \left[ P[x_L-x(s)] \right] \]
\[ = -w(L-s)\frac{dx}{ds} - P\frac{dx}{ds} \]
\[ = -w(L-s)\cos\theta - P\cos\theta \]
\[ = [-w(L-s)-P]\cos\theta \]

Substitute \(\frac{dM}{ds}\) back into \(\frac{d^2\theta}{ds^2}\), we get,

\[ \frac{d^2\theta}{ds^2} = \frac{1}{EI} [-w(L-s)-P]\cos\theta \]

For small angle \(\theta\), then \(\cos\theta=1\)

\[ \frac{d^2\theta}{ds^2} = \frac{1}{EI} [-w(L-s)-P] \]
\[ \frac{d\theta}{ds} = \frac{1}{EI} \int[-wL+ws-P]\,ds \]
\[ = \int \left( \frac{w}{EI}s - \frac{w}{EI}L - \frac{P}{EI} \right) ds \]
\[ = \int(as-b)\,ds \]

Where

\[ a=\frac{w}{EI} \quad \text{and} \quad b=aL+\frac{P}{EI} \]
\[ \Rightarrow \frac{d\theta}{ds} = \frac{1}{2}as^2-bs+C_1 \]
\[ \Leftrightarrow \theta(s) = \frac{1}{6}as^3 - \frac{1}{2}bs^2 + C_1s + C_2 \]

The initial conditions are

\[ \theta(0)=0 \]

We get

\[ C_2=0 \]

And

\[ \left(\frac{d\theta}{ds}\right)_{s=L}=0 \]

We get

\[ \Rightarrow \frac{1}{2}aL^2-bL+C_1=0 \]
\[ \Leftrightarrow C_1=bL-\frac{1}{2}aL^2 \]

Then

\[ \theta(s) = \frac{1}{6}as^3 - \frac{1}{2}bs^2 + s\left( bL-\frac{1}{2}aL^2 \right) \tag{*} \]

Given:

\(L=50cm\)

\(width=3cm\)

\(h=1cm\)

\(E=200GPa\)

\(w=11.55N\)

\(P=150N\)

\[ I = \frac{1}{12}width \times h^3 = \frac{1}{12} \times 0.03 \times 0.01^3 = 2.5\times10^{-9}m^4 \]
\[ EI = 200\times10^9 \times 2.5\times10^{-9} = 500Nm^2 \]
\[ a = \frac{w}{EI} = 0.0231 \]
\[ b = aL+\frac{P}{EI} = 0.31155 \]

Substitute into equation \((*)\), we obtain

\[ \theta(s) = 0.00385s^3 - 0.155775s^2 + 0.1528875s \]
